Sound pack: NK Cream (NovelKeys Cream)
========================================

Source
------
Mechvibes soundpack `nk-cream`
  https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes  (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 Hai Nguyen)
  path: src/audio/nk-cream/

This pack ships 35 individual per-key WAV files, so no slicing was involved
and there are no slicing artefacts to filter out.

License
-------
MIT. Compatible with this repository's MIT license.
Unlike the older packs in this directory (bubble / mechanical / drum),
no CC-BY-NC material is involved.

Files
-----
1.wav .. 8.wav   eight ordinary key samples  (from e g i n r s t u)
space.wav        upstream space.wav
enter.wav        upstream enter.wav
backspace.wav    upstream backspace.wav

Processing applied
------------------
1. decoded to mono / 44100 Hz / 16-bit PCM (canonical RIFF, fmt + data only)
2. leading and trailing silence trimmed, so a keystroke fires immediately
3. the 8 generic keys chosen are those whose peak was closest to the pack median
4. every one of the 11 files level-matched to -25 dBFS RMS,
   clamped to a -0.5 dBFS peak ceiling so nothing clips.
   This step is required: raw per-key levels in the source differ by over 10 dB.
